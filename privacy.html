Privacy Policy

Your privacy matters to us.

Last updated: September 10, 2026

1. Introduction

Welcome to Radix Point. We provide free online tools designed to help users convert, resize and compress files directly in their web browser.

This Privacy Policy explains how information may be handled when you use the Radix Point website.

2. Information We Collect

Radix Point does not require you to create an account to use our basic tools.

Files selected for our browser-based image tools are processed locally in your browser whenever the tool supports client-side processing. We do not intentionally upload those files to a Radix Point server.

If you contact us by email, we may receive information that you voluntarily provide, such as your name, email address and the contents of your message.

3. How We Use Information

Information voluntarily provided through contact messages may be used to:

  • Improve our services and user experience.
  • Identify and resolve technical problems.

These services may collect information according to their own privacy policies and terms.
