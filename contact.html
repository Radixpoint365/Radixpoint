🛠️ Technical Support

If a Radix Point tool isn't working as expected, please include the following information in your email:

  • The name of the tool you were using.
  • The type of device you are using.
  • The browser you are using.
  • A brief description of the problem.

If possible, you can also include a screenshot of the problem.

We aim to review and respond to genuine messages as soon as reasonably possible. Response times may vary depending on the nature and volume of requests.
