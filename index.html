🎓 How to Apply Calicut University Degree Certificate

Follow these 9 steps to apply for a Calicut University Degree Certificate:

  1. Open the official Calicut University Students Portal using the link below:

    👉 Open Students Portal
  2. Select Degree Certificate . It is the second option in the sidebar.
  3. Check your details carefully and proceed with the application.
  4. Upload your required documents:
    • Photo: 20–40 KB
    • Self-attested Plus Two / Diploma Lateral Entry Certificate
    • Provisional Certificate
    • SSLC Certificate
    • Consolidated Mark List
    Each PDF should be under 400 KB.
  5. Save the uploaded documents and click Proceed .
  6. Carefully double-check all the application details and proceed to payment.

    Ordinary: ₹400
    Urgent: ₹950
  7. Select your preferred payment method.

    UPI is recommended.
  8. A QR code will be displayed for payment.

    Use your UPI app to scan the QR code or take a screenshot of the QR code and complete the payment.
  9. After successful payment, download and print the application/payment receipt for future reference.
Important: Check the file size and document requirements carefully before uploading. Also verify all personal and application details before making the payment.
