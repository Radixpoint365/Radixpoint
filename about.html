About Radix Point

What is Radix Point?

Radix Point is a free online tools platform created to make everyday digital tasks simpler, faster and more accessible.

From students working on assignments to everyday users handling images and documents, Radix Point aims to provide useful tools without unnecessary complexity.

Our Mission

Our mission is simple: make useful digital tools available to everyone in one convenient place.

We focus on creating tools that are easy to understand, easy to use and accessible across smartphones, tablets and computers.

Built for Students & Everyday Users

Students often need quick solutions for assignments, projects, presentations and digital files.

Radix Point is designed with these everyday needs in mind while remaining useful for anyone who needs simple file and productivity tools.
